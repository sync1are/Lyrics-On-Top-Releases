# Lyrics On Top 🎵

A beautifully minimal, fully transparent, and always-on-top lyrics overlay for Windows. 

**Lyrics On Top** seamlessly syncs with what you're listening to—whether you're using Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, or even your web browser—and displays stunning, synchronized lyrics right on your desktop while you work or game.

## ✨ Features

* **Universal Compatibility:** Automatically detects the current playing song from any Windows media player without needing you to log in to Spotify or other services.
* **True Transparent Overlay:** Sits beautifully on your screen without getting in the way. 
* **Click-Through Mode (🔒):** Lock the overlay so your mouse clicks pass right through it, perfect for keeping it active while gaming or working.
* **Karaoke Mode:** Feel the rhythm with Spotify-style word-by-word highlighting that perfectly syncs to the beat.
* **Deep Customization:** Tailor the aesthetics completely to your liking via a sleek settings menu (⚙️):
  * **Themes:** Choose from carefully curated color palettes or select your own custom lyric font color.
  * **Typography:** Swap out fonts, adjust weights, and tweak sizing to perfectly match your desktop setup.
  * **Animations:** Select from fluid entry and exit animations (like Typewriter, Fade & Slide, or Pop & Scale).
  * **Layouts:** Choose between Single-line, Two-line, Three-line, or a striking typographic "Block Pattern" mode.
* **Ambient Visualizer:** A calm, atmospheric background visualizer steps in gracefully when listening to instrumental tracks or when no lyrics are available.
* **Zero Dependencies:** A truly standalone application. Install it, run it, and enjoy. No hidden requirements or complex setup.

## 🎛️ How to Use

When the app is running, hover over the right side of the lyrics to reveal the sleek control HUD:
* **⚙️ Settings:** Open the comprehensive settings menu to tweak the look and feel.
* **🔓 / 🔒 Click-Through Lock:** Toggle whether you can click on the lyrics or if your clicks should pass straight through to the windows behind them.
* **📌 Always On Top:** Toggle whether the lyrics should stay permanently pinned above all your other windows.

*You can also access the settings or lock the overlay by right-clicking the app icon in your Windows System Tray (bottom right).*
